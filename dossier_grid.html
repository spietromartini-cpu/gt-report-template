Methodology & Disclosures

Methodological Foundation

This dossier applies the GRID Value Model (GRID = Geospatial Risk & Investment Decisioning), developed by Pietro Martini (GT Global Estates & Capital) on the basis of the treatise From the Rubble and computed by the GRID Engine. The decision logic is TRI, VETO and MAO. A binary veto gate is checked first; if a knock-out criterion applies, no value is calculated. Otherwise the engine projects ten years of expected cash flows, including re-letting risk, and capitalises an exit value.

The discount rate is calibrated so that the same asset, fully let at market rent with a market-typical lease, is worth its net operating income divided by the market yield; the market value indication therefore follows broker yield evidence and differs only where this asset's income differs. Market rent is capped at the prime rent in broker research. The maximum allowable offer is the highest price, including purchaser's costs, at which the target return of the stated strategy is still reached after a legal risk provision for title risk above the market-normal level. Location quality moves yields and re-letting probability; geopolitical risk enters only the downside scenario.

Decision Aid & Limitation of Liability

This dossier is a decision aid based on information provided by the client and on public market data at the date of preparation. It is not a valuation under ANEVAR or RICS standards, not a legal or tax opinion and not investment advice, and it does not replace due diligence. All legal, cadastral, technical and tax points must be verified by qualified local professionals before any binding offer. To the extent permitted by law, GT Global Estates & Capital accepts no liability for decisions based solely on this dossier. A purchase decision should be taken only after your own in-depth review and a personal consultation.
