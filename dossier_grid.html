Methodology & Disclosures

Methodological Foundation

This dossier applies the GRID Value Model (GRID = Geospatial Risk & Investment Decisioning), developed by Pietro Martini (GT Global Estates & Capital) on the basis of the treatise From the Rubble and computed by the GRID Engine. The decision logic is TRI, VETO and MAO. A binary veto gate is checked first; if a knock-out criterion applies, no value is calculated. Otherwise the engine projects ten years of expected cash flows, including re-letting risk, and capitalises an exit value. Market value is the reconciled income and comparable value. The maximum allowable offer is the highest price at which the profile's target return is still reached after a legal risk provision derived from the Title Risk Index.

Location quality is not added as a surcharge; it moves yields and re-letting probability. Information advantages are shown as negotiation room, not as value. Geopolitical risk enters only the downside scenario, because market yields already contain country risk.

Limitation of Liability

Automated preliminary assessment based on information provided by the client and on public market data at the date of preparation. It is not a valuation under ANEVAR or RICS standards, not a legal or tax opinion and not investment advice. All legal, cadastral, technical and tax points must be verified by qualified local professionals before any binding offer.
